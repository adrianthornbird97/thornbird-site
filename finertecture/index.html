The problem

Most small businesses don’t fail for lack of hard work

They fail because nobody helped them see their cash, plan their growth or get ready for a loan. An experienced full-time CFO costs at least R150,000 a month. Going without one often costs more.

Will I make payroll on the 25th?A 13-week cash forecast, low-cash alerts and a weekly “safe to spend” figure.
Am I charging enough?Margin by product or job, a price-rise calculator and a quote-check template.
Can I afford one more person?A hire scenario: full cost to company, break-even sales and runway impact.
