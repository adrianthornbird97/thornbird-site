Questions

Can I change plans?

Yes. Upgrades start immediately, with the new fee from your next billing date; downgrades apply from your next billing date after the 3-month minimum. Change your plan.

Is my data confidential?

Your numbers stay yours. Every client's data is kept confidential under our terms and privacy notice. If you'd like extra peace of mind, tick a box at sign-up and we'll send you a mutual NDA that protects us both equally. It's optional and doesn't hold up your start.

How do I pay?

By card or debit order, monthly in advance, through a secure hosted checkout.

Which systems do you connect to?

Bank statements on Cashtecture (Foundation); read-only Zoho Books, Xero or Sage on Structecture (Structure) and Bluetecture (Blueprint).

Need more than Bluetecture (Blueprint)?

Thornbird’s Fractional CFO retainer covers management accounts, board packs and multi-entity work.
