Pricing

Three plans, one financial architecture

Prices are per month and are the full amount; no VAT is charged. Billed monthly in advance; 3-month minimum, then cancel any time.

Foundation

R1,650 /month
  • Mobile dashboard: cash, sales, margin, debtors and 8 KPIs, refreshed monthly
  • 13-week cash forecast
  • Weekly nudges and alerts
  • Ask-anything chat, 20 questions a month
  • Document upload by photo and the template library
  • 1 escalation to Adrian a quarter
Choose Foundation

Structure

R4,100 /month
  • Everything in Foundation, refreshed weekly
  • 13-week and 12-month forecast, weekly roll
  • Unlimited chat (fair use)
  • Read-only Zoho Books, Xero or Sage connection
  • Monthly live clinic with Adrian
  • 1 escalation a month; bot-drafted annual budget
Choose Structure

Blueprint

R8,250 /month
  • Everything in Structure
  • Scenarios: hire, price rise, loan
  • Monthly “owner letter”
  • Quarterly 60-min architecture review with Adrian
  • Priority, unlimited reasonable escalations
  • Annual budget and pricing review with Adrian
Choose Blueprint

Onboarding fee: R1,500 (Foundation) or R3,000 (Structure and Blueprint). Waived for partner referrals and pilot clients. Not included: bookkeeping, tax filings, payroll or financial-product advice.

Questions

Can I change plans?

Yes. Move up or down at the next billing date.

How do I pay?

By card or debit order, monthly in advance, through a secure hosted checkout.

Which systems do you connect to?

Bank statements on Foundation; read-only Zoho Books, Xero or Sage on Structure and Blueprint.
