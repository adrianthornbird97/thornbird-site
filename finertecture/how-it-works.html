What we don’t do

Your accountant stays your partner

Finertecture does not do bookkeeping or data capture, post journals in your books, file tax returns, run payroll or give financial-product advice. Bookkeeping stays with you or your accountant. When you need more, Thornbird’s Fractional CFO retainer is the next step.
