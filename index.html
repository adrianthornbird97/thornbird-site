System migration · How we work

Move to the cloud without losing a single transaction.

Leaving desktop or legacy accounting doesn't have to mean starting from scratch or trusting a rough import. We move your chart of accounts, opening balances and full transaction history from systems like Sage into Zoho Books or Xero. Then we check the new ledger against the old one, year by year, before we call it done. You get a clean, budget-ready system, plus reporting you can rely on from day one.

  1. Discovery and plan: we review your current system, entities, currencies and reporting needs, then choose the right platform and plan.
  2. Data extraction and mapping: we export your accounts, balances, customers, suppliers and history, and map them to a clean chart of accounts.
  3. Set-up and configuration: new organisations, VAT/tax settings, financial year, users and bank feeds.
  4. Full history load: opening balances and every journal, automated through the platform's API where possible, with safeguards against duplicates.
  5. Reconciliation sign-off: trial balances compared year by year between the old and new systems, with a written verification report.
  6. Go-live and handover: budgets loaded, reports set up, transactions locked, and your team and accountant trained.
Who it's forOwner-managed businesses, trusts and property or hospitality groups running one or more entities on ageing desktop accounting who want cloud access, multi-currency, budgets and real-time reporting, without losing their history or their auditor's trust.

Bookkeeping & accounting

Clean books, every month, without the chase.

Good decisions start with numbers you can trust. We keep your books up to date in Zoho Books or Xero, check every bank line rather than relying on automatic rules, and reconcile each account to the bank balance. Whether you run one company or several across different currencies and countries, you get accurate records and clear monthly reports, so you always know where your cash and profit stand.

Who it's forOwner-managed businesses, property and short-stay accommodation owners, family groups with several companies or trusts, and entrepreneurs with interests in more than one country who want reliable monthly numbers without hiring a full-time accountant.
  • Bookkeeping in Zoho Books and Xero, including setup, chart of accounts and clean-up of backlogs
  • Bank statement imports, line-by-line categorisation and monthly reconciliation to the bank balance
  • Multi-currency and cross-border accounting, including foreign-exchange transfers and intercompany balances
  • Invoices, bills, VAT/PDV records and refund tracking
  • Monthly management reports: income statement, balance sheet and cash flow, with year-on-year comparisons
  • Group views across related entities, with intercompany reconciliation

Tax planning

Tax planning that starts before the transaction

No nasty surprises: we plan them out before they happen.

The biggest tax savings are decided before a sale, distribution or year-end, not after. We model the options in plain numbers. That covers when to sell, how gains and income flow through a trust, and who should carry them. We bring in your existing tax adviser to confirm, so decisions are based on clear workings, not guesswork.

Who it's forFamily trusts, property owners and private investors facing a significant disposal or distribution, and the trustees and accountants who support them. It suits anyone who wants the options costed before deciding.
  • Capital gains tax calculations, including base cost, pre-2001 valuations and allowable disposal costs
  • Trust tax planning: vesting gains and income in beneficiaries versus keeping them in the trust
  • Timing of property and asset disposals across tax years
  • Using assessed losses and exemptions efficiently
  • Beneficiary-level tax modelling, scenario comparisons and adviser-ready workbooks and reports
  • Reconciling the tax figures to your accounting records (Sage, Zoho Books)

Strategic finance / fractional CFO

Turn your numbers into a plan you can act on.

We work alongside owners and boards as a fractional finance partner. We connect your long-term goals to the numbers that drive them: where cash really comes from, what the business can afford, and the steps that get you to where you want to be. We build clear forecasts and debt and funding plans, test the big decisions before you make them, and then track progress every month so the plan stays on course as things change.

Who it's forOwner-managed businesses, family groups and property investors with several entities, often across more than one country, who want CFO-level thinking without a full-time CFO and who want their numbers working towards long-term goals such as reducing debt, sustainable cash flow and building wealth across generations.
  • A long-term financial plan with objectives, milestones and measurable targets
  • A rolling 12- to 60-month cash-flow forecast with what-if scenarios
  • A debt-reduction and funding strategy, including refinancing and lump-sum options
  • Decision support for major moves: acquisitions, disposals, expansion or restructuring
  • A group and entity structure review across companies, trusts and borders
  • A monthly progress report and KPI dashboard against the plan
